# Website: Grabbit

Scope: every page `tools/website/build.py` makes. Home, download, IDM alternative and benchmark
are Persuade; help, FAQ, news and legal pages are Read and take the same world with restraint.
English only; light and dark, the visitor's system choice by default.

Visitor: a Windows user who wants a fast, free download manager, often coming from IDM. Action:
Download for Windows (installer from GitHub Releases). Proof: the real app at work, the public
benchmark against IDM 6.42, the price of nothing. Constraints: clean room (no IDM artwork);
no invented claims or social proof; every image is the real app. The product has passed IDM:
IDM is the benchmark's yardstick and a "moving from IDM" path, never the identity.

Memorable moment: the real app shown working on a centred stage, then pinned while the scroll
walks its jobs.

Unresolved: the app and its screenshots still say Mister Download Manager until the rename
(design/graphite/plans/10) ships; a 10-15 s screen recording replaces the hero still when it
exists; PRODUCT.md still carries the IDM-clone positioning.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Show, don't compare. One centred stage where Grabbit is seen doing the work, then the
scroll walks its four jobs while the stage stays put. It refuses the clone pitch (a static IDM
look-alike window under "works like IDM") and the dev-tool template (glow, gradient text, card
rows).

OWN-WORLD: Graphite at page scale: jet black #09080d / near-white #f7f6fb grounds, one raised
tray surface, hairlines with a top light edge; cyan (#1fe0f0 dark, #00788e light) is the single
accent, for Download and Grabbit's own numbers; amethyst only for links, focus, selection; the
Grabbit double-chevron mark carries the one gradient. Geist, tight tracking, tabular figures.
Pill controls, 24/16px nested shells, 8px windows.

STORY: The visitor sees the app working, reads that it is free and fast, watches it add,
download, torrent and fetch video, sees the measured times against IDM, and downloads.

GitHub). Centred headline about 80px, two lines, the second in muted ink; one 20px line under
it; the cyan "Download for Windows" pill beside a ghost "See the benchmark". Under them, the
stage: a 1080px double-bezel tray holding the real main window, fading into the ground at the
fold.
