What Grabbit does

Add from anywhere

Paste a link, drop a magnet, or click a download in your browser. A torrent opens Add torrent, where you pick the files first.

Eight connections at once

When one crawls or drops, the others take its share. A crash or restart resumes where it stopped.

Torrents in the same list

Peers and pieces in view, queued and scheduled like any download. No second program.

Video in the quality you pick

Paste a page from over a thousand sites and choose the format.
